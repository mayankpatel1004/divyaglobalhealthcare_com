Application Guide & Specs

5-Step Application Guide

1

Cleanse

Follow wound-care instructions provided by a healthcare professional.

2

Dry Skin

Ensure the surrounding skin is clean and dry before application.

3

Align Dome

Centre the raised frame opening directly over the wound area.

4

Secure Border

Gently press the adhesive border onto surrounding healthy skin.

5

Inspect

Check the seal and inspect the wound through clear window as advised.

2. Material Components & Functional Purpose

Component 1

Raised Ring / Frame Structure

High-resilience polyurethane / EVA medical foam structure. Provides the 5mm clearance required to prevent dressings or clothing from pressing into the wound bed.

Purpose: Shock absorption and structural integrity under compression.
Component 2 (R&D)

Biodegradable Ring (Future Line)

PLA / PHA bio-polymer composite under development to provide eco-friendly bio-degradation for disposable clinical waste without compromising physical strength.

Purpose: Sustainable medical waste disposal compliance.
Component 3

Skin-Contact Adhesive

Hypoallergenic acrylic or medical-grade silicone adhesive perimeter border ensuring firm skin fixation with pain-free, residue-free removal.

Purpose: Secure seal against bacteria while protecting delicate surrounding skin.
Component 4

Top Cover Transparent Film

Ultra-clear polyurethane (PU) membrane over the viewing window. Fluid-impermeable yet moisture-vapor permeable (MVTR).

Purpose: Continuous visual monitoring without dressing removal.

3. Intended Use & Clinical Focus

CLARIDOME™ is intended for post-operative surgical incisions, minimally invasive port sites, cosmetic surgery recovery, and sensitive skin lacerations where direct pressure, rubbing, or frequent removal of adhesive dressings causes pain or tissue trauma.

4. Validation Before Claims & Evidence Basis

All performance claims regarding breathability, non-touch protection, and adhesive skin safety are backed by ISO 10993 biological evaluation standards and physical testing data for medical film dressings.

Standards Referenced: ISO 10993-5 (Cytotoxicity), ISO 10993-10 (Skin Irritation/Sensitization), EN 13726 (Test methods for primary wound dressings).

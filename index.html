Why Medical Professionals Choose CLARIDOME™

  • Zero Contact Protection: Suspends dressings above the wound bed to prevent painful sticking and re-injury upon removal.
  • Sterile Micro-Ventilation: Micro-perforated air channels allow moisture evaporation while blocking external dirt and liquid.
  • Clear Visual Monitoring: Transparent dome enables clinical staff to inspect wound status without disturbing the dressing.
  • Skin-Friendly Adhesive Rim: Dermatologically tested, latex-free medical adhesive ensures firm hold with painless removal.

5-Step Standard Application Guide

1

Cleanse Area

Sanitize skin surrounding the wound per clinical standard.

2

Peel Backing

Remove protective adhesive strip liner carefully.

3

Position Cap

Center dome directly over wound without touching tissue.

4

Secure Rim

Press adhesive ring gently against skin to seal intact.

5

Monitor Clear

Inspect wound healing visually through clear cap dome.

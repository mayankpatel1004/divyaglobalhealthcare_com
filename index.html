Innovation Spotlight

Why Choose CLARIDOME™?

Traditional dressings often adhere to fresh sutures, creating severe discomfort during removal and increasing friction on delicate wound edges. **CLARIDOME™** introduces a raised resilient ring that suspends a top cover film safely above the wound.

  • Zero contact with central wound area
  • Transparent PU cover film for easy visual inspection
  • Soft silicone PSA adhesive beneath the ring

MATERIAL OPTIONS & PURPOSE

1

RAISED RING / FRAME

Medical-grade resilient PU foam or closed-cell PE foam. Die-cut central opening; prototype height 5 mm. Select density and compression resistance to maintain wound clearance. PU and PE are not inherently biodegradable.

2

BIODEGRADABLE RING — R&D OPTION

Cellulose/chitosan-based porous composite candidate. Not a validated substitute for the structural frame. Verify wet strength, shape retention, degradation and skin-contact safety.

3

SKIN-CONTACT ADHESIVE

Soft silicone PSA or medical acrylic PSA beneath the ring. Advanced option: silicone-to-skin / acrylic-to-frame double-coated PU carrier. Select adhesion for intended wear time.

4

TOP COVER FILM

Medical-grade PU / TPU film. Transparent preferred for viewing; translucent option reduces visibility. Specify moisture-vapour transmission, liquid barrier and mechanical strength. Keep adhesive outside the wound window.

5

ASSEMBLY & PACKAGING

Compatible film-to-frame bonding; silicone-coated release liner; protective pouch. Sterile-barrier pouch if supplied sterile.

Core Application

INTENDED USE

Proposed raised cover to maintain space above selected superficial wounds or closed incisions, subject to clinical assessment. Frame and adhesive contact surrounding intact skin; no absorbent pad in the central window.

Subject to professional medical supervision and incision site evaluation.
Regulatory Compliance

VALIDATION BEFORE CLAIMS

Biocompatibility • compression and film sag • adhesion • seal / liquid barrier • moisture-vapour transmission • sterilization compatibility and shelf life if sterile.

No claim of shockproof performance, faster healing, microbial barrier or whole-product biodegradability without finished-product evidence.

Standard clinical testing required for custom non-standard sizes.

Evidence basis: Winner Medical—medical PU foam; Avery Dennison Medical—PU film and silicone/acrylic adhesives; published cellulose/chitosan biomaterial research. Material options are alternatives, not all layers combined.

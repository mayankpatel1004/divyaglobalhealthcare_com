Our Vision & Mission

Revolutionizing Wound Protection with Purpose

Divya Global Healthcare was founded on the principle that surgical recovery should be painless, hygienic, and accessible. Over the years, our R&D teams have focused on addressing a persistent problem in clinical settings: post-surgical wound contamination and painful dressing adjustments.

By developing non-touch protection technology like **CLARIDOME™**, we enable doctors, surgeons, and nurses to protect delicate surgical sites without compromising patient comfort.

"Our mission is twofold: to set new global benchmarks in surgical wound protection, while driving social inclusion by empowering Divyang talent across our supply chain."

Key Features & Clinical Advantages

Standard gauze and adhesive tape often press directly against fresh sutures, causing agonizing pain during dressing changes and increasing wound tear risks. **CLARIDOME™** creates a protective, ventilated dome shield over the wound site.

Clear Rigid Protective Shield

Optically clear dome enables visual wound monitoring without dressing removal.

Aerated Ventilation Micro-Ports

Permits healthy air circulation to dry incisions rapidly while stopping dirt ingress.

Hypoallergenic Medical Adhesive Rim

Gentle skin adhesion suitable for sensitive skin, elderly patients, and children.

CLARIDOME™ Sizing & Technical Specifications

Standard Size Cap

Ref: CD-STD-01
  • Dome Diameter: 65 mm
  • Dome Height Clearance: 25 mm
  • Adhesive Border Width: 15 mm Hydrocolloid
  • Recommended For: Biopsies, Minor Sutures, Pediatric Procedures

Large Size Cap

Ref: CD-LRG-02
  • Dome Diameter: 95 mm
  • Dome Height Clearance: 35 mm
  • Adhesive Border Width: 20 mm Medical Acrylic
  • Recommended For: Orthopedic Sutures, Abdominal Incisions, Large Grafts
Clinical Protocol

Simple 5-Step Application Process

1

Cleanse Site

Disinfect surrounding peri-wound skin thoroughly and pat dry.

2

Peel Liner

Remove sterile backing paper from the hypoallergenic adhesive border.

3

Center Dome

Align transparent dome directly over sutures without touching wound.

4

Press Edges

Gently press down adhesive border to create a secure, comfortable seal.

5

Monitor & Recover

Inspect wound healing through clear dome without needing to remove cap.

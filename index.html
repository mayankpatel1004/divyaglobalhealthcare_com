5-Step Application Guide

1

Clean Area

Clean and thoroughly dry the surrounding skin around the wound.

2

Peel Backing

Peel off the protective adhesive release liner without touching the dome interior.

3

Center Dome

Center the raised CLARIDOME™ directly over the target wound or incision.

4

Press Edges

Firmly press down along the adhesive perimeter border to secure a firm seal.

5

Monitor Safely

Observe wound healing through the crystal-clear dome without needing removal.

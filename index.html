Who We Are

Dedicated to Safer & Painless Wound Care

Divya Global Healthcare is an innovative medical devices and healthcare company specializing in modern non-touch protection solutions. Our primary goal is to alleviate patient pain during wound healing, reduce infection risk, and assist healthcare professionals with state-of-the-art protective dome technology.

Through continuous research and collaboration with medical professionals, our flagship product, CLARIDOME™, has transformed standard wound care protocols across surgical, dermatological, and emergency care settings.

CLARIDOME™ Non-Touch Wound Protection Cap

Clear, Rigid, Non-Contact Protective Shielding for Optimal Healing

Patented Technology

Revolutionary Non-Touch Shield

CLARIDOME™ is designed to bridge the gap between traditional stick-on gauze and exposed healing. By creating a physical, ventilated dome over sensitive skin areas, it prevents clothing, bedsheets, or accidental touches from disturbing the wound bed.

Clear Inspection

Transparent dome permits quick clinical assessment without removing the shield.

Painless Removal

Eliminates scab tearing, exudate sticking, and dressing friction discomfort.

Easy Usage Protocol

5-Step Application Guide

How to properly secure CLARIDOME™ for maximum protection and hygiene.

1

Cleanse Wound

Sanitize the wound bed and surrounding skin according to standard medical advice.

2

Position Cap

Center the CLARIDOME™ over the target site ensuring full clearance over the lesion.

3

Apply Adhesive

Secure the wide flexible flange using medical tape or hypoallergenic adhesive strips.

4

Visual Monitor

Inspect recovery status periodically directly through the transparent dome surface.

5

Safe Replace

Gently lift tape edges when replacing. Zero contact with sensitive central wound area.
